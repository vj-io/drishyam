Inspect the bridge photo public/samples/bridge_03.jpg with the drishyam MCP tools. Start with open_image and overview, then gaze at the strongest candidates and segment each gaze. Decide every next call from the numbers the tools return: when a segmentation's entropy is above 0.5 or its mask is tiny, gaze one level finer at the same centre; verify a new mask and read its IoU (below 0.5 means the two looks disagree). Log the findings the segmentations show. Call request_human_review for every critical finding (exposed rebar, or spalling of 40,000 px² or more) and for every disagreement, giving the measured reason. In the why argument of every gaze, verify, enhance, log_finding and set_budget call, cite the measured value that led to the call. You cannot approve or reject findings; a person does that. Finish with draft_work_order and a short summary of what you found and what waits for a human. Use at most 25 tool calls.
